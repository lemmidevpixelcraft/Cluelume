2. Overview

Cluelume is a word and logic game. You do not need a separate Cluelume account to play. Cluelume does not operate its own cloud account service and does not ask for your name or email address for normal gameplay.

3. Game data stored on your device

Cluelume stores settings and game progress locally on your device. This may include:

  • language, sound/haptics and display preferences
  • statistics, Lume, streaks, missions and high scores
  • Daily Trio and Weekly Journey progress
  • Lume Atlas, Sigils and Prisma Archive progress
  • hints already revealed and onboarding state

This data is used to provide the game features. It is generally removed when the app and its local data are deleted, subject to any backups or separate Apple services managed by the operating system.

4. Advertising – Google AdMob

The free version of Cluelume uses Google AdMob for banner, native and interstitial ads. Cluelume Pro removes ads from the user experience.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information including IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device identifiers, advertising data, product interaction data, diagnostics and performance data. The exact processing depends on factors such as region, device settings, consent choices and ad configuration.

Where required, Cluelume uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent and privacy messages. Ad requests are made only when the SDK indicates that ads may be requested under the current consent state.

5. Apple Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you use Game Center, Apple processes the information required for Game Center features, such as player identifiers, scores, leaderboards and achievements. Cluelume receives the information provided by GameKit for these features.

Apple Privacy

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Cluelume Pro may be offered as a one-time lifetime purchase and as an optional auto-renewable monthly subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Cluelume does not receive your credit card or full payment details. The app receives only the product and transaction information required to unlock and restore purchases.

Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in your Apple ID subscription settings.

7. App Store and update services

Cluelume may contact Apple services to compare the installed version with a version available on the App Store. This involves the normal technical connection data generated when accessing Apple services.

8. Support

If you contact us by email, your email address and the content you provide are processed so that we can respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information that is not necessary for support.

9. Legal bases and retention

Where the GDPR applies, processing may be based on performance of a contract / provision of the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), legitimate interests in operation, security and support (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), or your consent where consent is required, especially for certain advertising processing (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Local app data is generally retained until you delete the app or its data. Data processed by Apple or Google is subject to those providers' retention practices. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for handling, documentation and ordinary organizational purposes.

10. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and objection, and you may withdraw consent for future processing. Contact lemmidevpixelcraft@gmail.com.

If you are in Austria, you can also contact the Austrian Data Protection Authority.

11. Children

Cluelume is not specifically directed to children as a “Kids” category app. If minors use the app, applicable legal and platform requirements also apply.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated when features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.
