Cluelume Support

Help with gameplay, purchases, Game Center and technical issues.

Contact

Support: lemmidevpixelcraft@gmail.com

For technical issues, please include your app version, iOS version, device model, and a short description of the steps that lead to the problem. Screenshots are also helpful.

Restore Pro

Open Cluelume Settings or the Pro page and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID that was used for the original purchase.

Manage monthly subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On iPhone, open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions to change or cancel Cluelume Pro.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If it does not connect, check that you are signed in to Game Center in iOS. Cluelume remains playable without Game Center.

Daily Trio / Weekly Journey

Daily and weekly content is based on the current calendar date. If something looks wrong, verify that your device date, time and time zone are correct.

Advertising

The free version contains ads. Cluelume Pro removes advertising. If an ad is displayed incorrectly, please send a screenshot and the approximate time to support.

Report a word or clue

If a word, translation or clue appears incorrect, send the language, theme and, if possible, a screenshot. This helps us identify the exact entry.
